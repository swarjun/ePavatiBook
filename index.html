ePavatiBook

Easy receipts & reports for NGOs, institutions and doctors

ePavatiBook helps organisations such as NGOs, trusts, institutions and clinics create and manage receipts (pavat). Enter the payer's details, choose a receipt type, and generate a clean, shareable and printable A5 PDF in seconds — on your phone, tablet or desktop.

What you can do

  • Create numbered receipts with automatic or manual numbering.
  • Keep a directory of your donors, patients or customers.
  • Filter and export reports to PDF or Excel.
  • Work fully offline; sign in to back up and sync across devices.
  • Use the app in English, Marathi (मराठी) or Hindi (हिन्दी).
