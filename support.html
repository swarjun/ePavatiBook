Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can use ePavatiBook without signing in — all your receipts stay on your device. Sign in only if you want to back up your data and sync it across multiple devices.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. ePavatiBook stores everything on your device, so you can create and print receipts without an internet connection. If you are signed in, your data syncs to the cloud the next time you are online.

How do I back up my data?

Sign in with email & password or Google Sign-In. Your organisation profile and receipts are then backed up to the cloud automatically and can be restored by signing in on another device.

How do I create and share a receipt?

On the Receipts tab, tap the + button, fill in the payer's details and amount, and save. Open a saved receipt to generate an A5 PDF that you can print or share via WhatsApp, email or any other app.

Can I change the app's language?

Yes. ePavatiBook supports English, Marathi (मराठी) and Hindi (हिन्दी). You can change the language from the app settings.

How do I export a report?

On the Reports tab, filter by date range, place or receipt type, then export the filtered list as a PDF or Excel file.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Account tab and tap Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page. This permanently removes your account and its cloud data.

What does Prime include?

The free version is fully functional and shows banner ads. Prime removes ads. Any additional Prime features are described in the app.
